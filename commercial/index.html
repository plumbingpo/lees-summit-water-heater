Safety

Code-Compliant Installation and Documentation

Badly installed or neglected hot water equipment can create carbon monoxide hazards, scalding, bacterial growth in lukewarm storage, and costly water damage when a tank fails. That's a real liability for any commercial property owner responsible for employees, customers, and tenants. Our commercial installations and inspections cover venting, combustion air, gas line sizing, relief valve discharge, expansion tanks, mixing valves, and code items like drain pans and seismic straps where required, and each visit ends with a written report listing risks, priorities, and recommended preventive steps.

This work serves a dual purpose: it protects people and property, and it provides the documentation that demonstrates due diligence to landlords, insurers, and health or building inspectors. We maintain detailed service logs, temperature records, relief valve test dates, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent service for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, laundries, and central plant rooms.

Emergency Service

Rapid-Response Water Heater Repair for Businesses

Every hour without hot water costs your business money. For a restaurant, it can shut down the dish room. In a salon, it cancels appointments. For a hotel, it brings complaints at the front desk, and in an apartment building it leads to tenant disputes. Lee's Summit Water Heater offers fast emergency water heater repair for commercial properties throughout the area, with after-hours scheduling for planned work so you don't lose a business day.

No two commercial emergencies look the same. It might be a failed gas valve, a burned-out element, a flame sensor fault, a tripped high-limit switch, a leaking tank, a stalled recirculation pump, or a tankless bank stuck in lockout. Whatever it is, we diagnose it fast, restore hot water, and record every step for your service records. Afterward, we put maintenance in place to keep it from happening again.

Preventive Care

Commercial Water Heater Maintenance Programs

Preventing failures is the most cost-effective way to manage commercial water heaters. Every Lee's Summit Water Heater program is customized to the property's equipment, usage, and budget, and covers scheduled inspections, flushing, anode rod checks, tankless descaling, relief valve testing, burner and vent inspection, recirculation and mixing valve checks, and temperature readings at the fixtures.

Compared with an emergency water heater replacement, scheduled maintenance typically costs a fraction as much, and it significantly reduces the risk of downtime and failed inspections. Businesses that commit to a program usually see fewer outages, longer equipment life, and lower fuel bills. Give us a call to talk through a plan for your property and budget.
